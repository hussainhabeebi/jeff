Is It Right for You?

Who Are Clear Aligners For?

Clear aligners work best for mild-to-moderate crowding, small gaps and many bite problems. They suit teenagers and adults who can wear their aligners consistently every day.

If your case involves severe rotation or major jaw movement, our orthodontist may recommend braces instead – we will always give you an honest recommendation.

FAQ

Clear Aligners in Al Nahda – FAQs

What are clear aligners? +
Clear aligners are a series of custom-made, transparent, removable trays. Each set gently moves your teeth a little further until they reach the planned position – without brackets or wires.
Are clear aligners as effective as braces? +
For mild-to-moderate crowding, gaps and bite issues, clear aligners are as effective as braces. For severe rotation or major jaw movement, metal or ceramic braces may work better. Our orthodontist will tell you honestly which suits you.
How long does clear aligner treatment take? +
Mild cases often take around 6–12 months. Your orthodontist will give you a personal timeline after your assessment.
Can I eat normally with aligners? +
Yes. You remove your aligners to eat and drink anything other than water, so there are no food restrictions. Brush before putting them back in.
Are clear aligners noticeable? +
Clear aligners are nearly invisible. Most people will not notice you are wearing them, which is why they are popular with adults and professionals.
Can I pay for aligners in installments? +
Yes. You can spread the cost of clear aligners with 0% installment plans through Tabby and Tamara.
