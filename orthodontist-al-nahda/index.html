Orthodontist in Al Nahda – FAQs

Where can I find an orthodontist in Al Nahda? +
Sahara Medical Centre is in Ganda Tower, Al Nahda, Sharjah, right on the Dubai–Sharjah border. Our specialist orthodontist Dr. Dynamol treats patients from Al Nahda Sharjah and Al Nahda Dubai, about 5 minutes away from Al Nahda 1 and 2.
How much does an orthodontic consultation cost? +
An orthodontic consultation at Sahara Medical Centre is AED 49. It includes an assessment of your teeth and bite, X-ray review and a personalised treatment plan.
Do you treat adults as well as children? +
Yes. There is no age limit for orthodontic treatment. We treat children, teenagers and adults, and many adults choose ceramic braces or clear aligners for a discreet result.
Which orthodontic treatments do you offer? +
We offer metal braces, ceramic (tooth-coloured) braces, clear aligners and fixed or removable retainers. The orthodontist will recommend the best option for your teeth at the consultation.
Can I pay for braces in installments? +
Yes. You can spread the cost of braces and aligners with 0% installment plans through Tabby and Tamara.
What are your clinic timings? +
We are open every day from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM, including weekends, so it is easy to fit appointments around work or school.
